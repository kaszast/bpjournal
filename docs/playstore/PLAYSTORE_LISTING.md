```text
Take control of your cardiovascular health with BPJournal!

BPJournal is a clean, modern, and clinically focused blood pressure diary designed for quick, effortless daily tracking. No intrusive ads, no forced account sign-ups: your health data stays strictly on your personal device or securely syncs with Android’s official Health Connect.

🩺 WHY CHOOSE BPJOURNAL?

• Fast Input with Wheel Pickers:
No tedious typing. Smooth scrollable wheels let you log your Systolic (ST), Diastolic (DST), and Pulse (BPM) values in seconds, along with measurement arm, body posture, and context tags (resting, post-medication, stress, etc.).

• Clinical Standards (ESH / ESC Guidelines):
Every reading is instantly classified according to the official European Society of Hypertension thresholds (Optimal, Normal, High Normal, and Grade 1–3 Hypertension).

• Clear Visual Analytics & Trends:
Monitor your cardiovascular dynamics with intuitive 7-day trend charts, category distributions, and daily/weekly/monthly aggregated statistics.

• Doctor-Ready Medical PDF Reports:
Preparing for your next doctor\'s appointment? Generate a structured, printable A4 clinical PDF report complete with averages, distribution charts, and measurement logs at the tap of a button. Easily share it with your physician or cardiologist.

• Health Connect & Google Fit Integration:
Enjoy automatic synchronization with Android’s official Health Connect ecosystem, seamlessly uniting BPJournal with smartwatches and health platforms.

• 100% Privacy & Offline Capability:
Your medical data is yours alone. BPJournal operates entirely offline without third-party analytics or external cloud accounts.

• Custom Daily Reminders:
Stay consistent with customizable morning, midday, and evening notification reminders.

• Full CSV Data Export:
Export raw records anytime into standard CSV spreadsheets for Excel analysis.

Empower your health journey with BPJournal — simple, elegant, and reliable.

*Medical Disclaimer: BPJournal is designed for logging and analyzing blood pressure readings. It does not measure blood pressure directly and is not a substitute for certified medical devices or professional medical advice.*
```
